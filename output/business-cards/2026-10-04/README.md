# Wallace Shift business card options

Three concepts for standard 3.5 × 2 inch landscape, double-sided business cards. Each board shows Bella's front/back on the top row and Matthew's front/back on the bottom row.

## 01 — Glass & Water

Closest to the homepage: glass identity over dark water, graphite contact side, serif names, and restrained azure accents. Recommended for the strongest connection to the site's signature hero.

![Glass & Water](/Users/matthewwallace/Repos/WallaceShiftLogoSite/output/business-cards/2026-10-04/01-glass-and-water.png)

## 02 — Pearl Editorial

Pearl brand face with the existing tagline in prominent serif typography; dark contact side with clear Manrope-style names and a single azure rule. The most restrained option.

![Pearl Editorial](/Users/matthewwallace/Repos/WallaceShiftLogoSite/output/business-cards/2026-10-04/02-pearl-editorial.png)

## 03 — Architectural Split

Silver and blue-glass identity inspired by the About scene, paired with a pearl contact face and a strong graphite diagonal. The boldest graphic option.

![Architectural Split](/Users/matthewwallace/Repos/WallaceShiftLogoSite/output/business-cards/2026-10-04/03-architectural-split.png)

## Files and production status

The three final PNGs are 1536 × 1024 pixel presentation boards, not individual print-ready card faces. Their card proportions are visual approximations of the intended 7:4 trim ratio. The v1 files are superseded proportion studies, retained for provenance.

Built-in image_gen was used for all generation and revisions. Exact prompt sets are saved in `prompts.md` and `revision-prompts.md`.

Names, roles, email addresses, domain, spelling, logo appearance, and clipping were visually reviewed. Generated lettering and logo treatments are concept artwork; exact vector logo geometry, embedded brand fonts, actual-size readability, color output, trim, bleed, and printer specifications must be finalized in production artwork after design selection. The preview board must not be submitted directly for card printing.

Production target: 3.5 × 2 inch trim, with the chosen printer's bleed requirements (typically 0.125 inch per side), safe text margins, and separate front/back files for each person.
