## 01-glass-and-water

References: /Users/matthewwallace/Repos/WallaceShiftLogoSite/wallace-shift-logo-white-transparent.png, /Users/matthewwallace/Repos/WallaceShiftLogoSite/public/media/wallace-shift-hero-poster.webp

Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: Wallace Shift business card concept presentation, for a design selection.
Primary request: Design a sophisticated double-sided standard US landscape business card system for Bella Wallace and Matthew Wallace that exactly belongs to the existing Wallace Shift website.
Input images: Image 1 is the actual brand logo silhouette and must be preserved; any additional image is a website material/mood reference only.
Presentation: A clean flat-lay design board, wide landscape canvas, four equally sized card faces in a precise 2-column by 2-row grid, all perfectly straight-on, no perspective or rotations. Each card rectangle must have exactly a 7:4 width-to-height ratio (3.5 inches wide by 2 inches tall), square corners. Top row shows Bella's front and contact back; bottom row shows Matthew's identical brand front and his personalized contact back. Keep generous board gutters but let cards occupy most of image. Pale cool gray presentation surface. Tiny understated row labels outside the cards: "BELLA — FRONT / BACK" and "MATTHEW — FRONT / BACK". Board title at top and "3.5 × 2 in · double-sided" subtitle.
Brand constants: Palette ink #080d12, graphite #121a21, pearl #edf1f2, silver #c3d0d8, azure #4fa8ff, deep azure #175c99. Match Manrope sans-serif with refined Instrument Serif accents; use crisp highly legible type. Exact supplied angular logo: connected angular zigzag left body and separate slanted right stroke, two shapes, preserve spacing/proportions. Never replace with ordinary W/S letters.
Exact front text: "WALLACE SHIFT" and "Design. Technology. Momentum."
Exact Bella back text: "Bella Wallace", "Sales and first contact", "bella@wallaceshift.com", "wallaceshift.com".
Exact Matthew back text: "Matthew Wallace", "Engineering and design", "matthew@wallaceshift.com", "wallaceshift.com".
Both backs also show a small exact logo and the small line "Websites · Web apps · Automation". Names are the primary information. Keep contact details readable with realistic 8–10pt equivalent minimum and 0.16 inch safe margins. No invented phone numbers, no address, no QR codes, no icons replacing words. Text verbatim. No extra copy within cards.
Materials: Printed designs on opaque premium card, very subtle realistic paper/shadows at most. Reflections and glass are printed artwork, not transparent acrylic card stock.
Avoid: gold, orange, warm cream, neon rainbow, illegible text, overdecoration, tiny crowded details, invented logo shapes, rounded-corner cards, perspective views.
Board title: "01 / Glass & Water"
Design direction: Closest to the website's dark cinematic water hero. Front is full-bleed deep ink, the actual logo rendered as a luminous cool glass sculpture with restrained silver/azure refraction and a faint dark water reflection across the bottom quarter, calm smoky atmosphere. Logo occupies upper/middle center, not too large; small precisely tracked WALLACE SHIFT below, elegant small Instrument Serif italic tagline low on front. No busy waves behind text.
Contact backs are nearly solid graphite with very subtle cool reflected light confined to far right edge, pearl names set large in refined Instrument Serif, Manrope role in silver, small flat white logo upper right, a short azure horizontal rule under name/role block. Lower area has email and website clearly aligned left in pearl, services as small silver footer. Premium editorial restraint; the artwork and hierarchy feel like a miniature extension of the website.

Board title: "03 / Architectural Split"

Front is graphite with the exact logo large on the right half, its left connected body appearing brushed silver and its separate right stroke appearing cool optical blue glass, subtle lit edges. Precise angled shapes preserve the exact logo rather than making a new monogram. Left side carries WALLACE SHIFT and small Instrument Serif italic tagline, plenty of open ink space. Cinematic but clean, no water. Back is pearl #edf1f2 and uses a bold solid graphite diagonal panel occupying only right quarter, its boundary angle taken from logo stroke; small white exact logo inside panel. All contacts remain entirely on the pearl left 3/4 with dark ink type. Names in Instrument Serif; roles and contacts Manrope; one tiny deep azure dot/rule. Strong asymmetric composition, striking but exceptionally readable at real business-card size. The same material theme is consistent across both fronts and both backs.
