# Competitor evidence and implications

Reviewed November 15, 2026. These findings describe published offers and product documentation. We did not log into competitors' customer accounts, validate delivery quality, or obtain conversion rates, profitability, or retention data. An advertised feature is evidence of a promise, not proof of successful delivery.

## Footbridge Media: an included CRM is explicitly documented

The Contractor Marketing Program advertises $249/month without a long term contract. Its broad bundle includes a website, hosting, SEO, Google Business Profile work, a consultant, and its dashboard CRM. The published program supports the chatbot's central claim about CRM inclusion, although the chatbot alone should not establish scope or terms. [Program](https://www.footbridgemedia.com/contractor-marketing-program)

The dashboard documentation describes customer records, lead forms, appointment scheduling, invoices, quotes, and payments through Stripe. Its detailed feature article explains that form submissions create or update customer records using email matching, and that booking creates appointments and customer reminder emails. This is materially more than an analytics screen. These are vendor descriptions of concrete workflows; we have not exercised them. [Dashboard](https://www.footbridgemedia.com/dashboard-standalone), [Documented workflows](https://www.footbridgemedia.com/marketing-tips/5-features-footbridge-dashboard-home-services-crm)

Cancellation provides HTML website files, but access to its dashboard, forms, CMS, review system, and hosting ends with the subscription. Website portability therefore does not mean ownership of the operating software. The FAQ says Analytics and Search Console accounts belong to the customer. A dedicated marketing contact is included. [FAQ](https://www.footbridgemedia.com/faq)

## 180 Sites: CRM automation is not a bundled CRM license

Starter is $195/month, Deluxe $250/month, and Premium $300/month. Deluxe includes ten website pages, automated SMS, and “CRM Automation.” However, the linked CRM product page explains the capability as moving website lead data into the customer's existing CRM. It lists connections to products including Jobber, Housecall Pro, Salesforce, and Zoho. This does not establish that a standalone CRM subscription is included. [Pricing](https://180sites.com/pricing/), [CRM integration scope](https://180sites.com/upgrades/crm/)

Standard CRM connection setup is included with Deluxe and above. Customers provide access to their accounts; 180 Sites describes using its own premium Zapier account. More advanced automation requires a separate setup quote. This distinction matters: a simple connection service is both narrower than a full CRM and more concrete than an unspecified dashboard benefit. [CRM integration scope](https://180sites.com/upgrades/crm/)

The initial term is a 24 month website payment plan, making Deluxe $6,000 across that term. The agreement transfers assignable website rights after full payment, with exclusions for third party and preexisting intellectual property. Hosting remains with 180 Sites during the term. Afterward, management automatically continues at $150/month unless cancelled. Included updates do not mean unlimited new development. [Published agreement](https://get.180sites.com/180-sites-agreement/)

## Contractor Growth Network: a different scope and customer

CGN's pricing page targets established design build remodelers, describing customers with $2 million to $7 million in revenue. Websites start at $15,000, with an advertised average around $26,000. Recurring SEO programs range from $3,150 to $6,400/month with an initial 12 month commitment. They include content, authority work, reporting, and strategy support. These scopes are not equivalent to a $250 website subscription. [Pricing and scope](https://www.contractorgrowthnetwork.com/pricing/)

Its listed inclusion is “Custom Portal & Performance Reports.” We found no explicit CRM license or contact management workflow in that pricing evidence. Do not convert a reporting portal into an assumed CRM inclusion. This is a limitation of the reviewed public evidence, not a claim that CGN never offers CRM work.

## Korravo: borrow the demonstration structure

Korravo makes its purchase concrete through a connected sequence: employee interviews, task maps, process maps, ranked AI opportunities, reports, and an advisor. Its examples explain recognizable work and the output the buyer receives. The product page labels the fictional Westbrook company and its examples as illustrative. [Product walkthrough](https://korravo.ai/product/)

Wallace Shift can use the same communication discipline: show an example website, a visitor inquiry, where that inquiry appears, and the agreed follow up. Label simulations clearly. Keep prices and questions accessible, and show only capabilities verified in our implementation. Competitor inclusion does not justify promising a client ready CRM: account separation, customer access, support effort, data export, and operating costs must support that promise first. Account connections for reporting must also remain distinct from paid campaign management.
