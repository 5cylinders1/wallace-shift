# Examples first: one focused revision

Reviewed November 15, 2026. This is an isolated local preview and a packaging recommendation. It does not replace the homepage, change the existing offer pages, deploy software, or establish improved sales.

[Open the interactive preview](http://127.0.0.1:5184/prototypes/homepage/refined/index.html). Detailed source evidence: [capabilities](./CAPABILITIES.md), [account connections](./CONNECTIONS.md), [competitors](./COMPETITORS.md), [verification](./README.md).

## Decision

Keep the two purchases clear. Include the existing private portal for the client's **Wallace Shift agreement and invoices** as an administrative convenience. Do not advertise a customer CRM or connected reporting dashboard as included today.

The software has reusable reporting adapters, protected billing records and useful demonstrations. It does not yet have a customer ready workspace combining those pieces. Ordinary clients are deliberately denied access to the owner reporting dashboard. A guide or setup fee cannot substitute for customer isolation and access.

## Setup, support and economics

Reusable work includes components, reporting adapters, document models and guides. Each customer still needs a scoped inquiry destination, suitable account permissions, sender configuration, delivery checks, handoff and support. A separate dashboard also needs isolated infrastructure and access, property selection, credential configuration, metric reconciliation and an agreed exit process.

Including agreement and invoice access reuses the current engagement administration. It still consumes record storage, invitation email, billing support and any payment processing fees. No measured per client figures were available, so this is not a claim of zero cost. The reported 30 to 60 minute website build time excludes scope, content collection, revisions, account setup, support and ongoing maintenance unless separately measured.

For now:

1. Include ordinary inquiry routing and launch checks in the website delivery. Include the agreed follow up setup and handoff in the $1,500 delivery.
2. Give customers instructions for their agreed tools, ownership and access responsibilities. Do not call the current owner configuration a self service connection product.
3. Quote optional extra connection assistance only for named, supported tools and deliverables after compatibility and access are known. Reporting customer access must be separately isolated and proven first. Do not advertise it as a ready add on through this preview.

A setup quote should list access checks, configuration, verification, handoff, a defined correction period and exclusions. A suitable pricing method is **estimated delivery hours × the chosen service rate, plus disclosed direct costs and a stated complexity allowance**. Check the resulting gross margin against loaded labor, infrastructure and expected support. Do not invent a flat fee before timing an isolated installation. Record renewals and repair work separately; an initial setup fee does not fund indefinite API maintenance.

Reporting code exists for GA4, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Facebook Page, Instagram professional accounts, LinkedIn Ads and Search Console. This means named read operations exist, not that a buyer can connect these through the portal today. Meta Ads combines Facebook and Instagram advertising totals. Content insights can include paid content. Personal Instagram accounts, campaign management, ad creative editing, provider lead imports and customer OAuth onboarding are unsupported in this product. Exact permissions, provider approval requirements and renewal limits are in [CONNECTIONS.md](./CONNECTIONS.md).

## Structure and communication

The preview keeps the current dark ink, pearl surfaces, blue accents, Manrope text, Instrument Serif emphasis, logo and existing demo media. It adopts Korravo's communication sequence rather than its styling or claims: a tangible output, recognizable work, an explanation of the mechanism, and a clear purchase. [Korravo product walkthrough](https://korravo.ai/product/).

The section order is:

1. **Opening:** “Make your work easy to choose.” Website demonstrations sit beside the two prices, package comparison and open question route. On mobile the prices precede the demo, within the first screen.
2. **Interactive inquiry journey:** website, project details, agreed follow up and human handoff. Every simulated state is labeled; no messages are sent.
3. **Two packages:** intended customer, price cadence, four core inclusions, visible additional cost disclosures, scope details and links to the existing offer pages.
4. **How work happens:** agree the scope, review the working example, launch with a handoff. An accurate illustration of the existing agreement and invoice portal supports this explanation.
5. **Custom systems:** one distinct route for needs beyond the packages.
6. **Questions:** CRM scope, basic SEO, connections and costs remain accessible before qualification.
7. **Optional showcase:** the existing animation stays intact and opens only when chosen. In this preview the link opens the unchanged current homepage. Any future route migration should preserve it at a dedicated showcase URL; no migration is made here.
8. **Open inquiry:** email Matthew or use the existing contact page without first choosing an offer.

The demo websites demonstrate design work, not client outcomes. Their fictional brands and advanced feature boundaries are stated. No fabricated proof, testimonial, revenue uplift, time saving or integration claim was added.

## Acceptance and staged validation

Preview acceptance:

1. Desktop and narrow mobile visitors can find both prices and billing cadence, a relevant example and an open question route without waiting for or dismissing an entrance sequence.
2. Each price links to its package. Additional cost categories are visible before its CTA; unresolved scope and commercial terms are disclosed.
3. All example selectors, video controls, inquiry stages, reply simulation, portal tabs, scope disclosures and mobile navigation work. Keyboard progression retains focus.
4. Demonstrations are identified as demonstrations. No real contact details, messages, payments, account connections or advertising changes are needed.
5. The original homepage and its animation remain unchanged. The preview produces no production conversion events.

Business acceptance requires subsequent evidence. Keep the preview local until the commercial terms and real delivery workflow have been settled. When a version is deliberately published, record traffic source, landing page, offer interest, inquiry, fit, sales outcome and delivery/support time. Existing email inquiries can initially be tracked manually in a private sheet; do not assume the owner dashboard is a sales CRM.

Define a qualified inquiry consistently as a real prospect whose need fits an offered scope and who can plausibly buy it. Record undecided information seekers separately without excluding them from contact. Count **qualified inquiries per relevant visitor** first; then sales per relevant visitor, acquisition cost per customer, contribution after acquisition, and delivery margin. For subscriptions, measure actual paid months and support time rather than assuming a lifetime value. Avoid optimizing for form starts, demo plays or total inquiry volume alone.

First capture a comparable baseline with channel and device mix. Traffic volume and baseline conversion are currently unknown, so a meaningful controlled experiment cannot be promised. If volume supports it, calculate the sample needed for an economically useful lift before allocation and use a fixed decision rule. If volume is low, use a staged release, watch real questions and lost opportunities, and compare like for like cohorts cautiously. Neither a small before and after comparison nor this usability walkthrough proves a sales increase.

The smallest subsequent test is the opening composition: immediate example, prices and open inquiry route while preserving the rest of the buying path. Track inquiry quality as well as volume. No recruited testing panel is required to make this preview or collect real incoming evidence; paid usability testing is optional.

## What would change the recommendation

Bundle reporting only after isolated customer access, repeatable authorization, supported account verification, revocation and renewal handling have been exercised, and measured support fits the margin. Bundle a CRM only after actual persistent customer records and working sales or service workflows exist for that customer. Consider a fixed setup fee after timed installations reveal repeatable work and support costs.

Change the page emphasis if actual prospects repeatedly misunderstand the price, mistake a demo for an included capability, cannot choose a path, or produce fewer suitable sales after adjusting for traffic mix. Prefer the alternate presentation if an adequately measured comparison favors it. A more attractive screenshot alone would not justify that conclusion.
