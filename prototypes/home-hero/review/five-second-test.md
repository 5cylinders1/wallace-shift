# Homepage hero: five-second and first-click tests

For five owners of trades or local service businesses who have never seen wallaceshift.com. Test each person alone. Five people find most of the problems a test like this can find.

**Before/after:** to compare, run the same questions on the old hero with a different five people. Serve `origin/main` plus the trim (see `README.md`) or use screenshots from `review/before/`. The same person can't see both versions.

## Setup

- **Desktop test:** a laptop showing `review/after/desktop-first-screen.jpg` (or the live page at 1440 × 900), scrolled to the top.
- **Phone test:** their own phone if they will, on the live page or `review/after/iphone-first-screen.jpg`.
- Don't explain who Wallace Shift is. Don't point.

## Part 1: five-second test (desktop, then phone)

Show the screen for **five seconds**, hide it, then ask:

1. "What does this company sell?"
   - Pass: websites and/or lead pages, or "websites for businesses like mine".
2. "What does it cost?"
   - Pass: names either price: $250 a month or $1,500 once. Both is a strong pass.
3. "If you wanted to talk to them, what would you click?"
   - Pass: "Discuss your project", or the blue button.
4. "Anything confusing?"
   - Write down their exact words.

A screen **passes** if at least 4 of 5 people pass each of questions 1–3.

## Part 2: first-click test (phone)

Show the first screen and read: **"You want to talk to them about a website for your business. Tap what you'd tap."** Record their first tap.

- **Pass:** "Discuss your project".
- **Also fine:** "Business website & care". It leads to the details, then to the same form.
- **Wrong:** the header "Call" button (it works, but skips the form), the menu, the demo tabs, or scrolling first.

Then read: **"Now you want to see what the $1,500 one includes."**

- **Pass:** they tap "Lead page & automatic follow up" and land on its card.
- **Wrong:** "Discuss your project" or scrolling past.

A first click **passes** if 4 of 5 people get it right.

## Part 3: the form (phone)

After they tap "Discuss your project", read: **"You'd rather book a video call than get a phone call. Do that, but stop before booking."**

- **Pass:** they tap "Book a Google Meet" within 20 seconds.
