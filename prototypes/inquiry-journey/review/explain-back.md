# Inquiry section: explain-back test

For five owners of trades or local service businesses who haven't seen wallaceshift.com. Test each person alone. The question is whether they can say back what happens after a customer fills in the form, and which parts they would be paying for, without being told.

**Before/after:** to compare, give the same questions to a different five people on the old section. The old version's screenshots are in `review/before/`. The same person can't see both versions.

## Setup

- **Desktop:** the live page or the dev server at 1440 × 900, scrolled so "The website is the start." sits under the site bar. Let them scroll through the section at their own pace.
- **Phone:** their own phone if they'll use it, otherwise `review/after/iphone-step1.jpg`, `iphone-step2.jpg` and `iphone-delivered.jpg` in order.
- Don't explain anything. Don't point. When they stop scrolling, hide the screen.

## Questions

Ask them in this order and write down their exact words.

1. "In your own words, what happens after a customer fills in the form?"
   - Pass: names all three in order: the customer sends the request, the customer gets a confirmation (and follow up if they go quiet), and the request reaches the owner's inbox or team.
2. "Which part only comes with the $1,500 lead page?"
   - Pass: the confirmation or the follow up.
3. "How does your team hear about a new request?"
   - Pass: email, an inbox, or a tool they already use.
   - Fail: a text message, a phone call, or an app notification. Those were cut because nothing sells them.
4. "Who writes back to the customer in the end?"
   - Pass: "my team", "me", or "us".
   - Fail: "an AI", "a bot", or "Wallace Shift".
5. "Was any of that confusing, or did anything look made up?"
   - Write down their exact words.

The section **passes** if at least 4 of 5 people pass each of questions 1–4.
