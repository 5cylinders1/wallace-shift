# Package cards: 10-second card test

For five owners of trades or local service businesses who haven't seen wallaceshift.com. Test each person alone. The question is whether one look at a card tells them what they'd pay, what they're committing to and which package fits, without reading the comparison table or the agreement.

**Before/after:** to compare, give the same questions to a different five people on the old cards in `review/before/`. The same person can't see both versions.

## Setup

- **Desktop:** the website card at 1440 × 900 (`review/after/desktop-website.jpg` or the live page scrolled to the card).
- **Phone:** the lead card on their own phone, or `review/after/iphone-lead.jpg`.
- Show the card for **10 seconds**, then hide it. Don't explain anything.

## Questions

Write down their exact words.

1. "What would you pay today, and what after that?"
   - Website pass: $250 now, then $250 a month.
   - Lead page pass: $750 now, $750 when it goes live.
2. "Can you get out of it? How?"
   - Website pass: cancel in the first 30 days for a refund, or cancel later and it ends with the month.
   - Lead page pass: end it in writing and get back anything paid beyond completed work.
3. "When is it yours?"
   - Website pass: after 12 monthly payments.
   - Lead page pass: once both payments are made.
4. "Is anything not included in the price?"
   - Pass: domain and paid services (website), or hosting, software and message usage (lead page).
5. Show both cards, one after the other, for 10 seconds each, and ask: "You want a page for one ad campaign. Which one?"
   - Pass: the lead page.
6. "Was anything confusing, or did anything feel like too much?"

The cards **pass** if at least 4 of 5 people pass each of questions 1–5.
