A roofing website starts a conversation. This CRM demonstration by Wallace Shift shows how the next steps could be organized.

Open the storm map to explore sample weather and nearby properties. It is a search aid, not proof of roof damage.

Review a property’s contact details, record source, and weather context. Then add it to the follow-up list.

Record the contact outcome and an internal note, keeping the next step with the property.

Choose an inspection time and assign a team member. The sample appointment updates the property’s status.

The worklist and upcoming appointments show the change. Customer records and sample website inquiries are organized in the same workspace.

All records are fictional. No live weather or contact lookup runs, and no messages or calendar invitations are sent. The handoff to inspection is visible.
