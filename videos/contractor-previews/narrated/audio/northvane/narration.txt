This is Northvane, a roofing website concept by Wallace Shift. It helps homeowners understand the work and prepare for a useful first conversation.

The opening illustration walks through the roof replacement, showing how the work comes together.

The roof planner then turns choices into a budget. We are using its built-in sample house.

Adjust the pitch and choose metal. The roof area and planning range respond. These prices are illustrative.

Explore a payment scenario, with the monthly amount and total of payments visible together.

The project questions gather the roof concern, payment approach, and timing, before asking for contact details.

Those answers become a structured lead preview, alongside the estimate and payment already explored. Nothing is sent from this demonstration.

The distinctive feature is the connection: the homeowner’s choices travel with the conversation.
