Stillwater is a pool website concept by Wallace Shift. It connects backyard inspiration with a concrete starting point.

The opening transformation follows the build through the finished setting, controlled by the visitor’s scroll.

Compare a social gathering place, a quiet courtyard, and a more natural escape. Each choice changes the inspiration.

That style carries into a project planner. Add features, choose an ideal timeframe, and describe the space in your own words.

The site brings those decisions into a readable brief, ready to download for the first conversation.

Stillwater is fictional and the images are concepts. The brief stays local; nothing is sent. The visitor leaves with something specific to discuss.
