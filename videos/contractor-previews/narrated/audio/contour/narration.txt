Contour is a roofing website concept by Wallace Shift, built around clear decisions and a visual introduction to the roof.

Its interactive three-dimensional study offers a top view. This is an illustrative model, not a measurement of the visitor’s property.

The separate estimator lets a homeowner explore a sample roof and begin comparing the options.

Choose metal and the planning range updates. These are sample prices, not a contractor quote.

Use the estimate’s midpoint in the payment explorer. Change the term and compare both the monthly payment and total repayment.

The visitor then brings their roof concern, timing, and estimate together in a project brief.

This creates an unsent preview with the selected options and sample pricing clearly identified.

A visual explanation becomes a practical comparison and an organized starting point for the contractor conversation.
