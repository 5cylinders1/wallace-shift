What if a roofing website helped homeowners explore the decision before the first conversation? This is Velar, a concept built by Wallace Shift.

An architectural opening and material collection lead into a roof design studio.

Upload a photograph, then choose a material and finish. The photo is prepared locally, with consent required before sending it for generation.

We are using prepared sample studies here. Compare the original with slate, then explore the same home in metal.

Enter measured roof area and complexity to explore an illustrative planning range. The photograph itself does not measure the roof.

Those choices carry into a project brief, keeping the conversation connected. This demo prepares the brief locally; nothing is sent.

A separate sample project room shows how approvals, site updates and documents could be presented after the sale.

From first impression to a more informed conversation. A tailored roofing experience, by Wallace Shift.
