Alder and Stone is Wallace Shift’s editorial roofing concept: a calm, guided path from understanding the work to planning a project.

The opening concept film explores preparation, protection, installation, and the final walkthrough. This is an illustration, not a completed customer job.

Next, the roof planner lets a visitor explore a sample property, without entering contact details.

Adjust the pitch and compare roofing materials. The planning range changes with those choices.

When they continue into a project brief, the estimate follows them to the next page.

The form gathers the property, roof concern, payment approach, and preferred timing in manageable steps.

The result is an organized, unsent request preview. The sample estimate stays clearly identified, and nothing is booked.

A considered visual identity, connected to a practical planning journey. Built by Wallace Shift.
