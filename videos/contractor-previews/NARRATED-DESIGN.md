## Purpose
Explain what Wallace Shift built, how one visitor journey works, and the distinctive interaction in each concept. The screen is the evidence; narration connects decisions and consequences. Eight varied beats for the Velar pilot, with duration driven by actual speech and interaction time. Other films use the number of beats their story needs.

Velar's real interface supplies midnight blue #152633, cream #f5f3ee and copper #c4a17d. Its Sora/DM Sans typography and original architectural films remain unchanged in captured footage.

## Framing
1920×1080. Large real browser footage fills the canvas, with a compact Wallace Shift/demo identifier and chapter label. A high-contrast caption band is reserved at the foot of frame. No permanent sidebar. Wide shots establish the experience; close views show the input and resulting value. Frame important content above the caption band. Use purposeful, modest pans and crops, never distort a UI control. Show the actual output long enough to read the highlighted consequence.

## Motion
Begin with the real architectural motion. Use 0.2–0.35s overlapping crossfades and subtle GSAP reframing between context and detail. Caption entrances are calm and quick. Cursor movement must correspond to real browser actions. No decorative motion competing with a task, no slow-motion filler, no synthetic product UI.

## Sound
Natural narration at original pitch. The selected route is Higgsfield through the browser using the user's existing credits; no HeyGen generation or new paid purchase. Retain the actual exported take and the voice/model provenance shown by Higgsfield. Map complete sentences to verified source timestamps; never distribute words evenly or equate every silence with a sentence boundary. Following the user's Velar viewing feedback to keep the voice and tighten pacing, `narrated-pace-audio.mjs` makes a separate derived take with conservative sentence-boundary silence trimming and pitch-preserving 1.08× tempo. Retain natural within-sentence pauses and all spoken words, measure the output, and remap sentence captions through the edits. The original audio and timing remain recoverable. The local Kokoro helper remains optional, unused production tooling, and is not the selected route. A licensed, credited, quiet instrumental sits beneath speech. The final mix uses narration and a restrained music bed; visual motion and captions mark the interface actions. Music/audio mix must stay intelligible on a phone speaker.

## Review standard

Prepared roof studies, entered area, illustrative prices, fictional records and unsent forms remain explicit. Buyer comprehension remains unresolved until actual representative buyers participate.
