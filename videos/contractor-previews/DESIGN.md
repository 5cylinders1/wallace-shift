# Contractor preview films

## Overview
Use the existing Wallace Shift identity as a quiet editorial frame around recordings of the real demos. The interface remains the hero. Each film has three twelve-second chapters: context, action, and result. Preserve the original demo's typography, images, animation, and sample disclosures inside the recording.

## Colors
- Ink `#080d12`: frame, from contractors.css.
- Graphite `#121a21`: inset surfaces.
- Pearl `#edf1f2`: primary text.
- Silver `#c3d0d8`: chapter and disclosure text.
- Accent `#4fa8ff`: progress, chapter numbers, and restrained motion.

## Typography
Manrope 500/700 for captions and labels; Instrument Serif for the demo name. These are the contractor gallery's existing typefaces. Keep commentary at least 32px at 1920px output width; show one short thought at a time. Do not cover the tool with captions.

## Elevation
A thin silver border separates the real browser capture from the frame. Use no simulated browser chrome, floating device mockups, or invented UI.

## Components
Top identity rail, large captured-interface stage, chapter title, caption rail, persistent sample disclosure, three-part progress rule. Captures show actual actions, not recreated controls.

## Do's and Don'ts
Do use controlled GSAP entrances, brief crossfades, and gentle reframing only when they help attention. Do preserve legibility and the continuity from choice to result. Do keep all meaning available with sound off. Do label prepared sample studies and illustrative estimates.

Do not imply live AI generation, operational CRM integrations, real customers, guaranteed measurements, or confirmed quotes. Do not stretch a short gesture into a full film with decorative filler. Do not obscure disclosure text. Do not use autonomous decorative motion over a working tool.
