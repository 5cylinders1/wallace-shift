Stratum is a roofing website concept by Wallace Shift, built around one live, interactive model.

As the visitor moves through the page, the same house helps explain the roof as a system, including the layers underneath.

In the roof studio, change the roof form and adjust the pitch.

The model and its calculated measurements respond.

Choose a different finish and see its illustrative planning range alongside the roof area.

Switch to an aerial view to inspect the design, then carry that choice into an inspection request.

The selected material, geometry, and range arrive together in the brief, keeping those choices connected.

This is a sample home, and the request remains an unsent demo preview.

No inspection is booked.

An interactive explanation becomes a practical starting point for discussing a roof project.
