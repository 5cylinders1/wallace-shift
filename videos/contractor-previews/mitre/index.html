A complete room. A clear next step.

Website concept by Wallace Shift

The Mitre and Rift opens with a room that builds as you scroll.

Wallace Shift created this editorial website for a fictional cabinetmaker.

A continuous rendered sequence moves from the empty interior through cabinetry, stone, and lighting.

The visitor controls the pace.

Short chapters explain each stage, then the finished room brings the materials and proportions together.

The rest of the site makes that first impression useful, introducing kitchens, pantries, built-ins, stone, and light.

A clear process explains the journey from measuring and drawing through workshop construction and fitting.

Material details show the palette behind the kitchen, with a contact section providing the next step.

The imagery is rendered and contacts are placeholders.

The distinction is making the whole room, and the thinking behind it, easy to understand.
