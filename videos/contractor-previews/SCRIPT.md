# Silent, caption-led product demonstrations

No narration or paid synthesis is needed: the requested previews should work immediately with sound off. Captions are baked into the composition and also supplied as WebVTT for accessible playback. This intentionally replaces the optional voiceover step in the HyperFrames production workflow.

Each film runs 36 seconds, with three 12-second beats. The editable scenario manifest is the script and shot list for each individual concept. Each beat contains a short title, a sentence describing the observed interaction, a real DOM focus target, and actual browser actions. No form submits to outside recipients and no paid generation calls occur during capture.

Velar is the pilot: show the luxury concept, compare an existing prepared study to its original photograph, then change a material to show the local illustrative result. Identify the study as prepared throughout. Generation time is not represented or simulated.
