Wallace Shift Consultations

This is Wallace Shift LLC's private calendar connection for arranging consultations through its Reply365 phone agent. Wallace Shift builds websites, web apps, web automations, and AI integrations for businesses.

The connection lets an authorized Wallace Shift administrator link their Google Calendar so the phone agent can check available times, create an agreed consultation, request a Google Meet link, and invite the caller using their confirmed email address.

Callers do not need to connect their Google accounts. This connection is for Wallace Shift's own calendar administration.

What access is used

  • Calendar availability to avoid offering busy times.
  • Calendar settings and conference support to check whether Google Meet can be created.
  • Events on the connected administrator's owned calendar to create and confirm consultations, and to create and remove a temporary connection test event.
