Privacy policy

This policy covers Wallace Shift Consultations, the private Google Calendar connection operated by Wallace Shift LLC through Reply365. It applies to information handled by this calendar connection.

Information accessed and created

With the calendar owner's permission, the connection reads busy time ranges, calendar metadata such as timezone and Google Meet support, and the details of consultation events it creates or retrieves to confirm a booking.

When a caller agrees to a consultation, the connection sends Google the appointment time, timezone, caller's name and confirmed email address, and a consultation title and description. Google returns event identifiers, event details and links, and, when available, a Google Meet link. Events may contain private Reply365 reference identifiers used to associate them with the correct booking.

Connecting the calendar also checks access by creating a temporary test event without sending guest invitations, then attempting to delete it. A cleanup failure can leave that test event on the calendar. The connection requests no Gmail access and does not read email messages.

How information is used and shared

Calendar information is used to arrange and confirm consultations, prevent scheduling conflicts, provide meeting details, and support the connection. Google processes calendar requests and sends event invitations to the confirmed guest. Meeting details are shared with the organizer and invited caller.

Reply365 and its hosting and database providers process connection and booking records to operate the service. Availability and booking details spoken during a call can become part of the call transcript and can be processed by the service's voice and AI providers, including Twilio and OpenAI, to handle that conversation.

Wallace Shift Consultations' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Calendar information is used for the consultation features described here, not for sale or advertising.

Storage and retention

Reply365 stores the connection credential in encrypted form so it can obtain renewed access tokens. Booking records can include the provider response, event and meeting links, appointment times, and references connecting the booking to the call and lead. Calendar events also remain in Google Calendar.

Disconnecting the integration removes the saved connection credential and disables that integration. It does not automatically erase existing call or booking records, remove calendar events, or revoke the separate permission in the Google account. No automatic deletion period is applied by the calendar connection to those historical records.

Your controls and deletion requests

The calendar owner can disconnect Google Calendar in Reply365 and revoke the connection in Google Account connections. Revoking permission prevents future authorized access; it does not delete previously created events or stored booking records. Existing events can be managed in Google Calendar.

For access, correction, or deletion requests relating to this connection and its stored records, contact matthew.wallaceshift@gmail.com. Identify the relevant booking or account without sending passwords or OAuth credentials.

Changes to this policy

Changes will be published here. A new use of Google user data requires an updated disclosure and consent before that use begins.
