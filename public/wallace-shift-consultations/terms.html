Terms of use

These terms apply to Wallace Shift Consultations, the private calendar connection operated by Wallace Shift LLC through Reply365.

Authorized use

This connection is intended for authorized Wallace Shift administrators using a Google Calendar they own or have authority to connect. By authorizing the connection, the administrator permits the calendar actions described on the connection information page and in the Google consent screen.

Use the connection only to arrange legitimate Wallace Shift consultations. Keep the account and OAuth credentials private, and do not provide credentials belonging to someone who has not authorized their use.

Consultation bookings

A requested consultation is not a confirmed appointment until Google Calendar confirms creation of the event. A Google Meet link is available only after Google supplies it. Invitation delivery is a separate outcome and may be affected by the recipient's email service.

The organizer manages calendar availability and existing appointments. Contact Wallace Shift to request a change or cancellation. Disconnecting the calendar connection does not cancel existing consultations.

Data and account control

The privacy policy describes how the connection handles calendar and booking information. The calendar owner can stop future access by disconnecting in Reply365 and revoking permission in their Google account.
