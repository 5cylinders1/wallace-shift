# Owner business dashboard

Mode: Operate. This extends the existing client portal at `/client-portal?view=business`; it does not replace the client agreement and payment experience.

The owner needs to see business activity across systems, identify missing or delayed evidence, and give agents a reliable reporting context. Preserve the portal's Manrope typography, pearl surfaces, ink text, azure controls, and compact dividers. Use plain language and named sources rather than ornamental charts or invented sample results.

The overview prioritizes recorded payments, website visitors, session key event rate and average search position, with website and search trends, separate advertising account totals and current receivables below. Data coverage and operational observations follow the important measures. The sidebar separates Dashboard, Website Analytics, SEO Analytics, Email, Clients, Paid Ads, Social Media, Social Media Posting, Business briefing and Connections. Paid and social account summaries open dedicated reports with addressable report and account query parameters.

A compact 7/30/90 complete day selector sits beside the heading. Manual refresh is an icon button and automatic refresh is in a small disclosure. The requested dates, source timezone and retrieval time remain visible. Current operational balances and the 30 minute realtime window state their own scope. There is no previous period dataset or invented comparison. Ordinary refresh errors retain a visibly dated prior snapshot; confirmed owner access expiry removes it. Clients uses the independently authorized agreement records and remains usable during an aggregate reporting outage.

The source is the unit of trust. Each report includes connection state, check time, processing/freshness limitations, available-through date when known, and metric definitions. No connection is inferred from a configured identifier alone. Missing permission, unsupported metrics, incomplete data, failed upstream calls, and true zero activity remain distinct.

Agent briefings in this version are deterministic observations of the snapshot. They must not imply an AI model was called, a recommendation was executed, or an autonomous agent is running. The snapshot is read only. LinkedIn publishing has a separate navigation entry and owner operated composer, with a fixed Wallace Shift Page target, local media preview, a reviewed immutable draft, explicit Publish action, and recorded outcomes. Reporting controls must not imply that they refresh or publish a draft. An uncertain publish result asks the owner to check LinkedIn and never offers an automatic retry that could create a duplicate. Future sending, spending, and record changes need separate capabilities and audit records.

Responsive behavior: sidebar becomes a labeled Page select, controls wrap, and tables scroll within named containers. Each observed daily trend has exact values in an expandable table. Empty series retain their metric selector; missing dates break the line. Preserve visible keyboard focus, clear status text, recoverable errors, loading feedback, and honest empty states. Do not expose secrets or private message bodies in the dashboard or exports.
