# Wallace Shift keyword research and page targeting

Research date: October 31, 2026, America/Chicago. Scope: small-business AI integration, custom CRM development, workflow automation, and supporting website work; Greater Kansas City and remote clients.

## What the evidence supports

The strongest **business-fit targets** are AI integration services for small businesses, custom CRM development, and workflow automation services. The sampled results contain providers selling these services, with related language around implementation, consulting, existing tools, scope, testing, and support. Kansas City searches also return local providers offering implementation and automation. These findings support the search intent and page structure below; they do **not** establish which keywords have the highest search volume or produce the most leads.

This research did not obtain a Keyword Planner export, organic keyword-difficulty dataset, or current Wallace Shift Search Console query data. The user believes no Google account currently owns the Search Console property. The current signed-in account reports no access to the `wallaceshift.com` domain property; its picker shows an unverified `https://wallaceshift.com/` URL-prefix property. We have not established verified ownership, and setup is needed. No verification or sitemap submission was performed. Actual performance metrics remain unavailable until access is established and reporting data is available. See the [Search Console setup handoff](search-console-setup.md). Ahrefs and Windsor were discoverable but not installed, and no metrics were fetched from them. No monthly volumes, CPCs, ranking positions, growth percentages, or conversion estimates are invented. The search tool's results are a sample, not a controlled Google US ranking report. Competitor presence establishes relevant published coverage, not performance, market share, or easy ranking opportunities.

## Buyer questions to answer on service pages

These questions are editorial recommendations based on the inspected offers and Wallace Shift's actual scope, not a measured “People also ask” export.

- AI: What useful task should we start with? Can AI work with our current tools? What information will it access? How are outputs checked? What does implementation include? How are software and usage costs handled?
- CRM: When is a custom CRM justified? Can our existing CRM be improved? What records, permissions, and reports are included? How are migration and integrations scoped? Who owns and supports the system?
- Automation: Which process should be automated first? Does it need AI or ordinary rules? What happens when a connection fails? What stops duplicate follow ups? What does ongoing support cover?
- Websites: What is included in the website build? How do forms reach the right person or CRM? How does mobile work? What is needed from the business? What is separate system scope?

Describe examples as possible scoped work. Keep demos clearly labeled as samples. Do not borrow competitors' claims about savings, delivery speed, tool certifications, client outcomes, or fixed integration coverage.

## Measurement required to call keywords “best performing”

1. Establish ownership of the `wallaceshift.com` **domain property** in Search Console using the intended business Google account and DNS verification. The user believes no account owns it yet, and this session did not verify ownership; older notes are not evidence of current access. Follow the [setup handoff](search-console-setup.md), then export available query and page data with dates, country, device, clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position. If no earlier data is available, the first complete reporting period becomes the baseline. Google's [Performance report documentation](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en) explains the available measures.
2. For market demand, use a Keyword Planner historical-metrics export for the United States and a separate Kansas City target, with the date range and network saved. Google's [metric definitions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575?hl=en) explain that monthly searches include close variants, depend on settings, and are rounded; the competition field measures advertisers, not organic ranking difficulty.
3. A Google Trends comparison for AI consulting, AI integration, custom CRM, and workflow automation was attempted, but the public Explore page was inaccessible through the research tool. No Trends values were obtained. Even with access, [Google explains](https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4365533?hl=en) that the 0–100 scale is relative interest, and low-volume terms can appear as zero. It is not monthly search volume.
4. After deployment, compare comparable Search Console periods and track qualified enquiries by landing page. Separate impressions, clicks, contact attempts, received enquiries, and qualified opportunities. A higher-volume keyword is not automatically the better business target.
