## Evidence limits and omitted claims

- Facebook failed in web retrieval. A direct browser visit confirmed the page title but displayed a login overlay; no posts or first-party service statements were verified.
- BBB identifies Jason Booze as owner and a February 27, 2023 business start. Neither is needed for the demo copy.
- BBB lists an A+ rating but explicitly says the business is **not BBB accredited**. The demo uses no BBB badge or accreditation claim.
- Nears.me lists 20268 Pflumm Rd, Bucyrus KS 66013, with the shop down the side driveway. [Enigma](https://www.enigma.com/directory/ks/bucyrus/) corroborates the street address. These are third-party listings; current owner confirmation is unavailable.
- [September 29, 2026 local reporting](https://olatherecord.com/articles/spring-hill-planners-weigh-auto-shop-industrial-rezoning-thursday-mulouupc) describes a **proposed** Bucyrus Auto facility at 19072 N. Madison St., Spring Hill, associated with Jason Booze/BWP LLC. This is not evidence of relocation. The demo omits a precise address rather than treating either location as verified current operating premises.
- Nears.me lists weekdays 8 a.m.–5:30 p.m. and weekends closed. Hours have no inspected first-party confirmation and are omitted.
- Directory prose makes broad claims about ASE certification, warranties, pricing, all-make capability, and repair services. This templated material is insufficient to establish those claims; none is used as verified shop fact.
- Review totals differ between directories (12 versus 13). No review score, testimonial, turnaround promise, or customer outcome is used.
- No verified appointment inbox or booking system was established by this research. The demo must clearly distinguish local form interaction from delivered requests.

## Image provenance

The user supplied the root folder `Bucyrus Auto & Drivetrain` and described the images as found online. Supplying files establishes the intended reference material, but does not establish who photographed them, publication rights, customer consent, or whether each vehicle was serviced by the business. The original files should remain intact. Actual shop or vehicle photographs must not be used to assert unverified projects, services, or ownership. Generated hero vehicles are illustrative and must not be presented as customer work or the actual premises.

## Owner confirmation before business launch

Confirm operating address, hours, service scope, rights to selected real photographs, and appointment delivery destination. Confirm submission receipt with an end-to-end delivery test before replacing the demo form notice with a delivery claim.
