## Service content and boundaries

- **Water heaters:** tank and tankless installation, replacement, service, and maintenance. The business explicitly advertises same-day water heater service. [Water heaters](https://www.deercreekplumbing.com/water-heaters)
- **Residential work:** drains, sewer cleaning and repair, faucets, garbage disposals, showers, sinks, toilets, sump pumps and battery backups, gas lines, grinder pumps, new construction, remodels, and basement finishes. [Residential services](https://www.deercreekplumbing.com/residential-plumbing-services)
- **Sewer services:** camera inspection, trenchless repair, replacement, and cleaning. The sewer page advertises 24/7 emergency sewer service while general office hours are weekdays. Until confirmed, urgent copy should direct people to call for availability; do not advertise every service as available 24/7. [Sewer repair](https://www.deercreekplumbing.com/sewer-line-repair)
- **Other established service categories:** commercial plumbing and radiant heat. [About page](https://www.deercreekplumbing.com/about)

No public source defines a ZIP whitelist or a guaranteed service radius. The area checker must describe a location as within the Kansas City region or require confirmation; it must not promise service solely from an invented radius. The scheduler is a demo request flow unless a real destination and availability source are connected.
