Ask only the questions needed to trace this example:

1. How did the inquiry arrive, and how did someone know it needed attention?
2. Who was responsible for the first response and the next task?
3. Where could another team member see the status?
4. Was any information copied between tools?
5. What happened after the estimate was sent?
6. What stopped reminders when the customer replied, declined, or booked?
7. If a step failed, who would notice and what would they do?
