# Discovery content and design

## Organize the site around the new strategy

Discovery begins with your strategic plan, brand guidelines, analytics and existing content. I will meet with the project owner and up to five additional staff or audience representatives. Together we will identify the tasks that matter most to partners, advocates, policymakers, journalists, funders and the public, and agree how to judge whether the proposed structure supports them.

The resulting sitemap will make your focus areas and approach easy to understand while allowing a resource to connect to more than one issue. Navigation, page labels and clear organization identifiers will help visitors distinguish TechEquity Collaborative from TechEquity Action. Your team will approve the descriptions and any language needed to explain the entities' respective roles.

Discovery produces an approved sitemap, audience journeys, content inventory, migration list, integration register and delivery plan. I will recommend practical choices for the CMS, publishing workflow, search and analytics, explaining their operating costs and implications for staff.

## Write useful page copy

The fee includes copywriting for up to **12 key c3 pages**, with a combined allowance of **8,000 words**. The copywriter will work from TechEquity's messaging, interviews and source material. Your team will review the wording and retain final approval of policy positions, factual claims and descriptions of your work.

Two consolidated review rounds are included. The content audit will also identify existing pages to retain, combine, update or retire. Other migrated records receive formatting and structural cleanup; rewriting the entire archive is outside this allowance.

## Extend the existing identity

I will develop wireframes and visual designs for up to **10 reusable c3 page templates**, covering the homepage, focus areas, organizational information, research hub and resource detail, campaign and coalition pages, articles, and other agreed publishing needs. We will agree the final template list during discovery.

Design includes two consolidated review rounds, responsive behavior, and a practical style guide for staff. The imagery allowance covers up to **20 finished image treatments** for key pages, using your existing identity, collage photo style and drawn elements. Sourcing, editing and licensing are included. The proposal does not include an organizational rebrand or a new logo.

# WordPress development and migration

## Publishing and research resources

I recommend WordPress with a custom theme and reusable editing blocks. Staff will be able to update pages, resources, campaigns and organizational information without editing code. Editor roles, previews and revision history will support review before publication.

The research hub will support keyword search and filters for agreed fields such as focus area, resource type and publication date. A resource can appear in more than one relevant collection. Structured entries will hold summaries, authors, dates, links and accessible downloads where supplied or remediated within the agreed scope.

Campaign and coalition templates will support action alerts, sign on links, partner information and calls to participate. The base includes configuration of up to **four existing external services** through their supported forms, embeds, connectors or links, covering newsletter signup, donations and advocacy functions. Discovery will confirm providers and access. Building a replacement CRM, donation processor or advocacy platform requires separate scope.

# Accessibility and proposed team

## Accessibility throughout delivery

The agreed websites will be designed and tested to meet **WCAG 2.2 Level AA**. Work begins with semantic page structure, keyboard access, visible focus, usable forms, contrast and layouts that remain functional when text is enlarged. Staff editing patterns will include heading guidance, descriptive links and meaningful image alternatives.

Testing will combine automated checks with manual keyboard, zoom, reflow and screen reader evaluation. The independent reviewer will assess the approved templates and key user journeys, document issues and retest corrections. I will correct defects in the agreed implementation before acceptance and provide the test record and any remaining dependencies for your review.

External forms, embeds and supplied files will be assessed during discovery. Where a service prevents an accessible journey, I will propose an accessible alternative, such as an equivalent form or a supported provider route, for your approval before launch. An inaccessible dependency will not be marked as passing. Additional document quantities or a replacement external system require a written scope decision.
