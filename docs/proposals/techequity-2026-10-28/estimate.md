# TechEquity internal estimate

Prepared October 28, 2026. Internal planning record, not part of the buyer proposal.

## Evidence and scope basis

The primary RFP was read in full on October 27 at https://techequity.us/our-team/request-for-proposals-web/ and verified again in the in app browser on October 28. Its current scope, budget, and deadline match this proposal. Direct web and curl retrieval returned HTTP 403; browser viewing resolved access. The submission form was also inspected without entering or submitting anything.

The buyer allocates $80,000 to $100,000 for the c3 redesign and c4 migration. Our estimate is based on workstreams and defined quantities. It is not derived by filling the buyer's ceiling.

The proposal assumes 10 c3 reusable templates, 12 key pages and 8,000 words of new copy, migration of 150 c3 records, up to 50 document source pages converted to accessible HTML or remediated, up to 5 existing c4 pages, 20 finished image treatments, and 4 existing external service providers. These quantities are proposed limits, not verified inventories. Two consolidated review rounds are included for copy and design.

The contingency is approximately 15.34 percent of the planning subtotal. A 15 percent calculation gives $77,768.75; the commercial quote rounds to $78,000. The $100 owner rate is an estimating sell rate, not payroll cost. This table must not be described as a profit calculation. Supplier rates and hours are assumptions, not quotes or confirmed appointments.

At the low end, 410 owner hours, 65 specialist hours at $125, $1,000 direct costs and 15 percent contingency total $57,643.75. At the high end, 640 owner hours, 110 specialist hours at $175, $3,000 direct costs and 15 percent contingency total $99,187.50. The $78,000 quote is therefore only viable with the stated inventory, supported integrations and review allowances. A large migration, custom provider API work or substantial scanned document remediation could exceed it.

## Buyer budget presentation

The fee table distributes the fixed price across eight workstreams and both entities. C3 totals $72,000 and c4 totals $6,000. Copywriting is separately listed at $9,000. Image sourcing editing and licensing is separately listed at $4,000. These are allocated selling prices, not a statement that every supplier cost is billed directly. They include coordination and overhead allocated among workstreams.

Milestones are 20 percent at kickoff authorization, 30 percent after content and design approval, 30 percent after implementation and migration acceptance, and 20 percent at launch and handover. Payments total $15,600 + $23,400 + $23,400 + $15,600 = $78,000. Proposed invoices are net 15 days. Termination reconciles work and approved commitments against advances.

The optional care plan is $750 monthly for both sites, or $9,000 for 12 months, with hosting and four hours of minor work. Hosting is bounded at 20 GB and 100,000 combined monthly visits, subject to actual traffic review before contract. Extra approved work is $125 per hour. External subscriptions, domains and payment processing fees are excluded and remain organization owned.

## Staffing and capacity

This is not a comfortable completely solo delivery plan. An independent accessibility reviewer and nonprofit copywriter are included. Neither is appointed, quoted, or committed. Their credentials and availability must be confirmed before contract execution. The proposal states this explicitly and places ordinary supplier price variation within the fixed fee.

The main period is February 1 through July 15, approximately 24 weeks. Matthew's 410 to 640 hour range averages approximately 17 to 27 hours weekly, midpoint 22. More concentrated peaks are likely during design, migration and launch. Existing FAS, CAMWS and Westside proposals are not awards, but concurrent wins could exceed available capacity. CAMWS proposes 20 to 25 hours weekly through April; Westside proposes 15 to 20 hours weekly from May. FAS capacity and current Legacy work also need to be considered. Do not represent this resource calendar as already confirmed by Matthew.

## Open commercial matters

Confirm the real content inventory, document formats, traffic, hosting constraints, provider identities and subscriptions, image usage rights, approved analytics/consent choices, and whether c3/c4 may share components. Confirm specialist appointments and Matthew's resource calendar before contract. The RFP declines proposal period questions, so these remain explicit assumptions to discuss during finalist presentations.

The c4 site is described inconsistently as a splash page and a few pages in the RFP. The proposed limit of five existing pages accommodates that ambiguity. It is a light migration, not a second full redesign.

The 50 document page allowance is a proposed scope boundary, not a blanket exception to accessibility. The working migration list must agree which files can be published accessibly. Larger archives and inaccessible provider dependencies need a decision before the fixed scope is signed or implementation proceeds.
