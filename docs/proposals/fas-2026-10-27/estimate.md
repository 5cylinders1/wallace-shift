## Reason for the revision

FAS removed the microsite template and navigation element and extended the proposal deadline to November 6, 2026 at 5 p.m. Eastern. The former item C program layouts, Future of Science pilot, local program navigation, and sister-site navigation work are removed. Former item D becomes item C, covering footer editing, WordPress-managed redirects, and visible publication tags.

The revised posting still applies items A and B across four named properties. This estimate retains the explicit proposal exception that new media and social editing capabilities are implemented on FAS.org only. The three sister sites receive discovery review only under the base project. Their navigation is not being changed. FAS must approve the narrower implementation scope in writing. The estimate must not be represented as covering equivalent editing capabilities on all four properties.
