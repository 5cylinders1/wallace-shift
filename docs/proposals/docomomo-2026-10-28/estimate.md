The 26 hour volunteer assistance/training entry includes 20 hours of assistance, 4.5 hours of sessions and 1.5 hours of preparation/handover; documentation is also produced during CMS and coordination work. Document remediation depends on editable originals or practical HTML equivalents. Complex scans or diagrams must be identified before contract, not accepted as unlimited remediation at this price.

Revenue less modeled direct expenses is $21,300. This is approximately $73.45 per owner hour at 290 hours, $66.56 at 320 hours, and $59.17 at 360 hours. These figures are before taxes, general overhead, insurance and unrelated business costs. They are not net profit forecasts.

The optional $1,800 annual renewal includes base software licenses. Using the modeled $800 hosting/software annual cost leaves $1,000 before owner time and other overhead, or approximately $83.33 per hour at 12 care hours annually. It is a routine platform care offer, not unlimited support. Supplier and maintenance costs need confirmation; renewal is by agreement rather than guaranteed for multiple years.

## Scope dependencies

1. National Docomomo authorizes Neon access and the chapter eligibility/login method. Membership access is substantive required scope and cannot be reduced to a public link without explicit agreement.
2. Existing Google Maps registry is preserved as an external resource. No new GIS database or 9,000 property migration is included.
3. All approximately 150 pages remain accounted for. Volunteers complete remaining manual migration after ten developer examples, with up to 20 hours of assistance. Developer defects remain included regardless of that allowance.
4. Fifty source document pages across ten documents are included. Additional legacy remediation, video captions and complex third party limitations need a written responsibility and acceptance plan before contract.
5. First year hosting, base software and routine platform care are included in the $23,800. Existing national CRM, ticketing, video and domain charges are separate; any new external subscription must be disclosed before contract.
