# Website design and functionality

## Organize the collection around its readers

Planning will establish the content register, sitemap, migration plan and access requirements. A design board will show type, color, patterns, basic messaging, imagery and page treatments using Docomomo US/MN's existing logo and graphic standards. Two consolidated design review rounds are included.

Navigation will connect research, buildings and designers, news, events and support options. Homepage tiles will draw from selected research, news and events. Social links and supported embeds will connect visitors to the organization's existing channels.

## WordPress and volunteer editing

I propose the standard WordPress block editor, a maintained open source base theme and established plugins. Custom code will address necessary presentation and integration work. Reusable layouts and controlled fields will support volunteer editing. Administrator, editor and contributor permissions will restrict access to site settings, templates and plugins and will be documented and tested.

The fee includes up to **ten reusable layouts**: homepage, general information, research listing, research detail, news listing, news article, events listing, event detail, member resources and search results. Existing contact information and approved forms or external service links can be incorporated into these layouts. A new brand identity and original research or long form copywriting are outside this scope.

## Included site functions

| Function | Included implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Research and news | Editable pages and posts with images, video embeds, documents, categories and individual URLs |
| Events | Upcoming listings sorted by date, permanent event pages and an archive with recordings and supporting materials |
| Ticketing | Connection to one existing ticketing service through supported links or embeds, with a tested member early access workflow |
| Membership | One Neon connection for the agreed chapter membership rule and restricted resource area |
| Search and discovery | Site search over indexed website text, useful content categories, editable SEO titles and descriptions, sitemap and redirects |
| Analytics | One client owned analytics property with agreed privacy settings and basic event tracking |
| Existing external resources | Preserve approved donation, joining and store links and the current Google Maps registry embed or link |

This proposal preserves the current registry as an external resource. A replacement GIS application, bulk import of registry properties into WordPress, a new store or payment processor, and search within scanned documents are not included.

# Migration and member access

## Account for the full existing archive

The migration plan covers approximately **150 existing pages or records**: five organization pages, 50 research pages, ten news posts, 15 symposium pages and 70 past events. A preliminary review before contracting will confirm these allowances. The complete content register is part of paid planning. Normal variations within the overall quantity can be redistributed among categories. Material additions require an agreed scope adjustment.

I will test an automated import on representative pages, adjust the mappings, and use it wherever it preserves content reliably. The inventory will record the existing URL, destination, media, migration owner and review status. Each existing URL in the agreed inventory will either remain valid or redirect to its new destination. Redirects and links will be checked before launch.

| Responsibility | Wallace Shift | Docomomo US/MN |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content register and import | Inventory, sample import, automated migration and reusable layouts | Identify authoritative content and approve grouping |
| Pages needing manual transfer | Demonstrate the process, complete ten representative pages and provide up to 20 hours of migration assistance | Assign volunteers to transfer the remaining manual pages, with image descriptions |
| Accuracy and quality | Check layout, headings, links and migration status across the agreed inventory | Verify research, captions, dates, image rights and completeness |
| Documents and recordings | Preserve files and supported embeds; implement the accessibility work described on the next page | Supply source files, captions or approved equivalents where required |

Volunteer migration will use a written checklist and staging site. I will review submissions in batches and return clear corrections. The assistance allowance does not cap correction of defects in my own import or implementation. No page will be silently discarded because an automated import fails. If volunteer capacity changes, we will agree on staffing, schedule or additional work before proceeding.

## Connect access to current membership

The proposed integration verifies a signed in user's membership against the national Neon system and grants access according to one agreed chapter eligibility rule. It includes the member resource area, renewal guidance, loss of access when membership expires, and one early ticket access workflow using the selected ticketing provider's supported controls.

Before contracting, Docomomo US/MN and the national administrator must confirm the permitted integration method, account access, chapter eligibility fields, login approach and ticketing restrictions. I will validate active, expired and nonmember cases in staging before relying on the connection. Credentials will remain on the server with limited permissions, and the site will retain only the member data needed for access.

The fee assumes one national CRM instance and supported access to the necessary membership data. A full CRM migration, general bidirectional synchronization or a separate custom identity platform is outside scope. If approved access cannot support the proposed workflow, we will agree on an alternative and its cost before signing; an ordinary public link would not substitute for the required membership functionality.

# Accessibility testing and handover

## Agree on a testable standard

I propose **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** as the technical acceptance target for the new templates, migrated HTML content and complete public and member journeys. This gives the RFP's accessibility requirement a defined testing basis. Before contracting, a preliminary review will establish the responsibilities and allowances for legacy documents and recordings.

Testing will include automated checks across the agreed website inventory and manual checks of every layout, navigation, search, event discovery and member access. Manual review will cover keyboard operation, focus visibility, screen reader use, headings, labels, errors, contrast, zoom and responsive layouts. I will correct implementation issues and provide a findings and retest log.

An independent accessibility reviewer will examine the key layouts and journeys and retest corrections before launch. This review is included in the fixed fee. The reviewer has not yet been appointed and will be identified for approval before contract execution. Routine cost variation for this included review will be absorbed within the fee.

## Make archival material usable

The fee includes accessible HTML equivalents or remediation for up to **50 source document pages across ten priority documents**, selected with the committee for importance to visitors. Docomomo will provide editable originals where available and approve the accuracy of any conversion. Image descriptions for manually transferred pages and captions or transcripts for existing recordings will be supplied by the organization's volunteers; I will provide guidance and incorporate those materials.

The full inventory during paid planning will identify remaining documents, recordings and external services that need attention. We will record their owners and remediation priorities against the agreed allowances, and obtain approval for any additional work. Existing inaccessible files will not be described as conforming merely because they are linked from an accessible page. No content will be removed or archived without approval. Remediation of another provider's software and unlimited PDF or video remediation are outside the fixed scope.

## Test the site and train its editors

Release checks will cover current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, plus mobile Safari and Chrome, at representative desktop, tablet and phone sizes. I will check search, redirects, embedded media, ticket links, account permissions and the membership scenarios. The launch checklist will include a backup, a tested restore procedure, DNS changes and a rollback plan.

Three remote training sessions of up to 90 minutes each are included: migration training, editorial publishing, and administration and handover. Docomomo will receive an editing guide, recorded sessions with participant consent, account and license information, the content register, and the test findings. Editors will demonstrate routine tasks on staging before launch approval.

Defects in the agreed implementation reported within **90 days after launch** will be corrected without an additional fee or an hourly cap. New features, new content production and changes to external systems are handled separately.
