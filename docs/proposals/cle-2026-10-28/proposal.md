# Website scope and editing

## Plan around the decisions visitors make

I would begin with a working session with you and up to three other CLE representatives. We would review the current content and available analytics, identify the main audiences, and agree how visitors should find program details, apply, sponsor, or contact CLE. The first deliverables are an approved sitemap, content inventory, and simple wireframes for the main journeys.

The proposed structure covers the homepage, CLE overview, staff and board information, Leadership Mohawk Valley, application guidance, alumni and community impact, sponsors and partners, news or events, and contact information. Links to other CLE programs at MVCC will remain easy to find. The final labels and grouping will follow the content rather than a preset page list.

## WordPress and reusable design

I recommend WordPress because it supports the editing and ownership requirements without a proprietary platform subscription. I would build a responsive theme around CLE's existing identity, using the standard WordPress editor and reusable blocks. Two consolidated design review rounds are included. A new logo or organizational rebrand is outside this proposal.

The base fee includes up to **six reusable layouts**: homepage, program page, general information page, people or partner listing, news and events listing, and article or event detail. Staff can change text, images, people, program information, dates, links, and form fields through the editor. New layout types or changes to underlying behavior require developer work.

## Accessibility and release checks

The design and implementation target **WCAG 2.2 Level AA**. I will check contrast, headings, alternative text, link names, form labels and errors, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, zoom, and responsive behavior. Automated checks will be supplemented by manual keyboard testing and screen reader checks of navigation, forms, and representative page layouts. Supported browser checks cover current Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge, plus mobile Safari and Chrome.

Testing covers all six layouts and the content populated under this proposal. I will provide a findings log, correct issues in the agreed work, and retest those corrections before launch. Existing external tools will be reviewed for obvious barriers, with a usable alternative link or contact route where practical. Remediation of another provider's software, legacy PDF files, and formal accessibility certification are not included.

## Staff handover and acceptance

Two remote training sessions of up to 90 minutes each will cover publishing, images, people and program updates, forms, events, backups, and account access. CLE will receive a concise editing guide and a launch checklist. At final review, staff will demonstrate the routine updates, test both forms, and approve the public content and navigation. Launch follows written approval. Defects in the agreed implementation reported within 60 days after launch will be corrected without an additional fee or an hourly cap.

# Ongoing care and client control

## Optional hosting and care plan

The **$175 monthly plan** includes hosting with HTTPS, daily backups retained for at least 30 days, uptime monitoring, WordPress and plugin updates, security monitoring, and one hour of minor content assistance each month. Hosting will use an account owned by CLE with administrator access; Wallace Shift will receive delegated access. I will verify backup restoration during setup and check backup status as part of routine care.

Routine updates will be tested before release where a change could affect the site. Security alerts will be assessed and appropriate updates or protective action scheduled promptly. The plan includes investigation of site failures and restoration from a clean backup where practical. Major incident recovery, forensic investigation, and work on external systems require a separate approved estimate.

Support is provided Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Central, excluding U.S. federal holidays. I will acknowledge ordinary requests within two business days and an outage report within one business day. These are response commitments, not guaranteed resolution times. Continuous emergency staffing is not included. Unused content assistance does not roll over; additional approved work is **$100 per hour**.

The plan is optional and billed monthly from launch. The quoted rate is held for the first 36 months for this scope. CLE may cancel with 30 days' notice and arrange replacement hosting and support. CLE can also choose another host from the outset; provider charges would replace the hosting and care figures in the ownership estimate.

## Ownership access and transfer

After payment, CLE owns the custom theme work, design assets, and organization specific materials created for this project. CLE retains its content and receives administrator access to WordPress, hosting, domain, analytics, and any selected services. WordPress and other open source components retain their existing license terms. Any licensed images or external services will be identified with their transfer restrictions before use.

At handover or termination, I will provide the current site files, database export, source code, design assets, account inventory, and configuration notes through a secure transfer. One standard export and account handover is included without an exit fee. Work to rebuild the site for another platform or configure a replacement provider is separate, at $100 per hour with advance approval. CLE will receive the information needed to make that choice.

## Proposed payment terms

Implementation payments are **$3,240 at kickoff**, **$4,320 after approval of the populated staging site**, and **$3,240 after launch and handover**. Proposed invoice terms are 15 days. These terms and the final content inventory will be confirmed in a written agreement. The price is valid for 60 days, excludes applicable taxes, and assumes remote delivery. No travel or optional purchase will be incurred without prior approval.
