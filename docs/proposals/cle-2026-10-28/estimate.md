# CLE internal estimate

Prepared October 28, 2026. Planning analysis for Wallace Shift, not a buyer supplied budget or a vendor cost quotation.

## Price basis

The proposed fixed implementation fee is $10,800. It comes from the defined work below, not an attempt to match an undisclosed buyer ceiling.

| Workstream | Estimated owner hours |
| --- | --- |
| Discovery inventory sitemap and meetings | 11 |
| Design and two consolidated review rounds | 16 |
| WordPress templates editor controls and forms | 28 |
| Content population and redirects | 12 |
| Accessibility browser and functional testing | 14 |
| Training launch and handover | 9 |
| Anticipated warranty corrections | 6 |
| Total delivery and warranty allowance | 96 |
| Fixed fee contingency allowance | 12 |
| Total priced hours at $100 reference rate | 108 |

The six warranty hours are an estimating allowance, not a contractual cap. Proposed phase fees allocate the total by deliverable and do not claim to be time billing. The 12 hours of contingency cover ordinary review and implementation variation; new scope requires written approval.

## Scope assumptions

One WordPress website; 12 pages; six reusable layouts; 20 existing news or event records; 30 people or partner entries; two forms with eight fields each. Client supplies approved copy, images, rights, and access. Basic registration and payment links are included. No new portal, CRM, custom transaction engine, bulk PDF remediation, original copywriting, or brand identity engagement.

Existing site URL and content inventory remain unverified. The RFP is flexible on pages and features. The quantities above are proposed contractual limits, not claims about existing site size. Clarify before submission if possible; otherwise state them plainly and confirm at contracting.

## Operating cost model

Optional care: $175 monthly, $2,100 annually. Three years = $6,300.

Domain allowance: $25 annually, $75 over three years. Actual registrar cost may differ.

Implementation $10,800 + care $6,300 + domain $75 = $17,175.

No additional paid software is required by the proposed base configuration. New payment or registration service costs are not part of the base. Existing provider subscriptions and transaction fees are unknown and must be included in the ownership estimate if retained. The proposal discloses this gap rather than showing a misleading zero transaction cost.

Care plan pricing is a firm proposal, not a supplier quote. Before a contract, select a hosting arrangement that supports client account ownership and delegated access, daily backups, staging or equivalent safe update workflow, and sufficient recovery controls within the price. The plan includes one hour of minor content help as well as recurring technical care; its margin depends on a stable low volume site. Do not promise emergency coverage. Review any inherited security issues before accepting maintenance responsibility.

Optional standard hosted registration setup: $1,200 fixed for one workflow, one event or payment purpose, links and confirmation testing. Assumes about 10 hours plus two hours of contingency. Obtain actual provider pricing before acceptance, and calculate fees from anticipated transaction volume.

## Capacity and cash flow

January 4 through April 1 is about 12.5 weeks. The 96 hour estimate averages approximately eight hours weekly, with peaks during build and testing. No specific new weekly capacity confirmation was obtained for CLE. Confirm commitments before contracting, especially if CAMWS or FAS awards overlap. Prior proposals are not awarded work.

Kickoff payment $3,240; populated staging approval $4,320; launch and handover $3,240. Total $10,800. Care billed from launch, including the defect warranty period because ordinary hosting and maintenance are separate services.
