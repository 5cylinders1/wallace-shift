## Wallace Shift baseline

The current [offer source](../src/content/contractorOffers.ts) includes hosting, maintenance, basic SEO and inquiry capture in the $250/month website. Automated follow up is explicitly separate. The $1,500 service includes one focused page, one agreed workflow, testing and handoff. Hosting, platform subscriptions, message usage and ongoing management can cost extra.

Page counts, revision limits, minimum commitment and package specific ownership are not finalized in that offer source. The [starter agreement](client-portal/starter-agreements.md) is a draft with ordinary cancellation and defect correction provisions; it does not grant the new satisfaction refund proposed below. Do not silently interpret its correction obligations as a money back promise. Reconcile the final guarantee with the issued agreement and payment schedule before publication.

## Recommended satisfaction policy for review

Use the label **Proposed 30 day satisfaction guarantee** in the isolated preview. Give the two purchases distinct eligible fees. Do not use an unconditional guarantee badge on a live offer until the policy is adopted.

### Draft terms

1. **Start event:** The 30 calendar day period begins when Wallace Shift delivers an accessible, complete version of the agreed website or demonstrates the complete lead page and agreed workflow, and sends written notice that it is ready for review. Incomplete work does not start the clock. If SMS is in the agreed scope, required sender registration or verification must be approved and the workflow tested before this notice; page completion alone does not start the clock. Use one recorded delivery date, rather than a date dependent on the customer clicking an approval button.
2. **Website remedy:** The customer can choose correction within the agreed scope or a refund of the first $250 website service charge and cancellation of that package. To keep this promise coherent, the proposed billing arrangement collects the first charge when the complete review version is delivered and does not collect the next service charge before the guarantee period ends. This billing arrangement is a proposal, not current implemented billing behavior.
3. **Lead page remedy:** The customer can choose correction within the agreed scope or a refund of the paid lead page and workflow setup fee, up to $1,500. A deposit, if any, counts toward that same fee; it does not create an additional refund allowance. No recurring care service is automatically started.
4. **Request:** A written request through the existing contact route within the period is sufficient. Include the engagement name and desired remedy. Asking for a correction in time preserves eligibility while that issue is resolved. Do not require the customer to accept a repair before choosing a refund, or to wait until a staff member processes it to qualify.
5. **Coverage:** Satisfaction with the agreed delivered design, content and configured workflow. It does not promise leads, sales, rankings, traffic or ad performance. Requests for new scope remain separate from correction of the agreed scope.
6. **Excluded charges:** Customer paid advertising, domain registration, third party subscriptions and consumed message usage are outside the service fee refund. Any such expense must have been separately identified and accepted before purchase. Do not use a broad, undisclosed deduction for Wallace Shift labor or ordinary payment processing to reduce the advertised eligible refund.
7. **What ends:** A timely refund request pauses future covered billing while it is processed. A refund ends the covered service and prevents future Wallace Shift charges for that service. The refunded new site or workflow is retired through an agreed transition. Customer supplied content, domains, accounts and collected inquiry data remain the customer's; provide their export before retiring anything. Do not alter an existing customer website or other unrelated work. A refunded custom deliverable is not also an indefinitely supported free product. The exact license, export format and transition period must be settled in the agreement.
8. **Outside the window:** Ordinary written cancellation, ownership and defect correction terms continue to apply. The satisfaction period must not be described as eliminating those rights. The refund processing deadline, pre delivery cancellation rules, tax treatment, payment method and data transition period remain decisions to complete before issuing this policy.

Suggested short preview copy:

> Proposed 30 day satisfaction guarantee. Review the agreed work before deciding to keep it. The website proposal covers your first $250 service charge. The lead page proposal covers the paid setup fee, up to $1,500. Third party charges are separate. See the proposed terms.

This is a stronger and more inspectable proposition than an unexplained guarantee badge, but it changes billing and refund commitments. The live offer must not inherit it merely because the preview displays it.

## Commercial exposure and decision rule

Track each engagement's discovery, content collection, design, build, setup, testing, revisions, handoff, support and closeout hours. The user's 30 to 60 minute build estimate covers only one component. Add hosting, software, message usage, payment costs and acquisition cost actually paid by Wallace Shift.

For the proposed website policy, maximum direct service refund is $250 per refunded new engagement. For the lead setup policy it is the collected setup fee, capped at $1,500. A refunded customer also leaves sunk delivery, support, transition and acquisition costs; the fee cap is not a cap on commercial loss. No real refund rate or delivery cost series was available in this review.

Use these calculations, with actual recorded costs rather than assumed hourly earnings:

```
Expected contribution per new engagement
  = expected collected service revenue
  - expected refunded service revenue
  - delivery and support labor cost
  - direct operating and payment cost
  - customer acquisition cost

Refund reserve for a cohort
  = observed or explicitly assumed refund rate
  × eligible service fees collected for that cohort
```

Model a zero refund scenario, the observed rate when available and a worse case chosen explicitly by the owner. Do not present those scenarios as predicted outcomes. For the monthly package, assess a fixed observation period with actual retention; do not count an uncommitted 24 month customer lifetime. A positive initial conversion change that produces more refunds or negative contribution is not a successful improvement.

## Three bounded upgrade opportunities

These are service scopes supported by the existing website and follow up offer definitions, not newly built software features. [Website service scope](../src/content/serviceLandingPages.ts) already distinguishes additional pages and extensive writing. All three need a quote based on actual work; there is no evidence supporting a standard Wallace Shift upgrade fee yet.

| Upgrade | Customer need and eligibility | Deliverable and boundary | Pricing and cost basis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Additional service pages | An existing website customer adds a distinct service or needs an additional useful destination beyond the written page allowance. | Named pages using the agreed website design, supplied or separately scoped content, responsive and inquiry checks. A functioning base website does not require this purchase. | Project fee for incremental planning, content placement, build, QA and revision allowance. Identify any lasting hosting or maintenance increase explicitly; do not automatically increase the monthly fee for every page. |
| Ongoing content updates | A website customer needs regular service information or project updates beyond the agreed base allowance. | Named content types, a defined monthly request and review allowance, turnaround and handling of unused capacity. Technical maintenance and included basic SEO remain in the base scope. | Optional monthly scope based on expected writing, editing, review and administration time plus direct costs. Define the base allowance first. No unlimited work, rankings or marketing campaign promise. |
| Additional follow up scope | A website customer needs the separate workflow service, or a lead package customer needs a second distinct workflow beyond the one included. | A named compatible tool, capture event, messages, timing, reply handling, stop rules, test and handoff. Do not bill the lead package customer again for the first agreed workflow. Existing pages can be reused when suitable. | Quote incremental setup and verification labor plus direct fees. Identify recurring platform and message costs separately. Ongoing monitoring, later changes and provider maintenance require a separate written scope if requested. No supported tool compatibility is assumed before review. |

This creates room for additional customer value without raising the baseline solely to increase revenue. It follows the useful distinction in 180 Sites' upgrade presentation while avoiding a copied price, new core package or unnecessary monthly charge. The customer can purchase the useful base package and stop there.

## What would change this recommendation

Adopt a different refund cap or delivery milestone if timed real engagements show unacceptable contribution loss, customers cannot review the complete deliverable within the proposed window, or payment operations cannot honor the published promise. Change upgrade priorities when actual requests show demand for a different adjacent need and delivery/support costs are known. Do not add a bundled CRM or standardized account setup price until client access, data separation, supported connections and support effort have been rehearsed.
